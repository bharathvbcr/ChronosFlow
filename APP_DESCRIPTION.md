# ChronosFlow App Description

## Short Description

ChronosFlow is a daily planning app that turns your schedule, tasks, habits, medication reminders, focus sessions, and routines into a clear 24-hour visual plan.

## Full Description

ChronosFlow is a productivity suite built around the Chronos Dial, a 24-hour circular planner that shows the shape of your day at a glance.

Instead of scattering your tasks, calendar events, habits, focus sessions, medication reminders, and routines across separate tools, ChronosFlow brings them into one calm daily operating system.

Plan your day, protect your focus, track habits, remember important medication times, review what actually happened, and improve tomorrow.

## Key Features

### 24-Hour Chronos Dial

See your full day as a radial planner.

- View sleep, work, study, meals, breaks, routines, and focus blocks
- See current and upcoming blocks
- Drag, resize, and edit planned time blocks
- Spot gaps and conflicts quickly
- Overlay calendar events and fixed commitments

### Task Planner

Create tasks, prioritize them, and schedule them into real time.

- Tasks and subtasks
- Priorities and due dates
- Recurring tasks
- Convert tasks into time blocks
- Start focus sessions from planned tasks

### Habit Tracker

Build routines that fit your real schedule.

- Daily and weekly habits
- Flexible goals, such as 3 times per week
- Habit windows
- Streaks and consistency tracking
- Missed-habit repair suggestions
- Habit markers on the Chronos Dial

### Medication Reminders

Track medication times with clear reminders and acknowledgement.

- Medication schedules
- Precise reminders when needed
- Dose acknowledgement
- Missed-dose follow-up
- Refill reminders
- Optional notes

ChronosFlow helps track and remind. It does not provide medical advice.

### Focus Sessions

Turn planned work blocks into active focus sessions.

- Pomodoro or custom timers
- Foreground focus notification
- Android Live Update support for active sessions
- Distraction notes
- Completion history

### Mood and Energy Check-Ins

Learn when you work best.

- Quick mood, energy, stress, and focus check-ins
- Track patterns over time
- Discover better deep-work windows
- Improve future planning suggestions

### Sleep and Routine Planning

Build days around real personal rhythms.

- Sleep blocks
- Wind-down reminders
- Morning and evening routines
- Workout, study, and custom routines
- Planned versus actual comparison

### Daily Review

Close the loop at the end of the day.

- Compare planned time with actual time
- Review skipped tasks and habits
- Capture why plans changed
- Improve tomorrow's schedule

### AI-Assisted Planning

Use AI to suggest better days while staying in control.

- Generate a balanced day plan
- Repair overloaded schedules
- Find open deep-work windows
- Reschedule missed habits
- Explain why a plan was suggested

AI suggestions are shown for review before they change your plan.

## Why ChronosFlow?

Most productivity apps are lists. ChronosFlow is built around time.

The Chronos Dial helps you understand your day visually: what is fixed, what is flexible, what is overloaded, what is free, and what needs attention.

ChronosFlow is for students, professionals, caregivers, creators, and anyone who wants one place to plan, execute, and improve their day.

- Dynamic color
- Edge-to-edge layouts
- Phone, tablet, foldable, and desktop-window support
- Keyboard, mouse, trackpad, stylus, and touch-friendly interaction
